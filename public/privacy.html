3. How information is shared

  • Other users. Your first name, rating and gender are visible on trips and bookings. Your mobile number and the vehicle plate number are shared only between a driver and a passenger whose booking has been accepted, and only for that booking.
  • Family Circle links. A trip share link shows the passenger's first name, the route, departure time, the driver's live position, and "near pickup" / "arrived" timestamps. It never shows phone numbers or plate numbers. Links expire automatically.
  • Service providers. Data is stored with Supabase (database, authentication and file storage), emails are sent through Brevo, Google Maps displays maps, and Expo delivers push notifications. They process data on our behalf only.
  • Legal. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect the safety of users.

4. Retention

  • Driver GPS fixes are deleted after 7 days.
  • Chat messages are deleted 30 days after they are sent.
  • Trip share links are removed 7 days after they expire; notifications after 90 days.
  • Account, profile, trip and booking records are kept while your account exists. CNIC images are kept while you are a verified driver.

5. Your choices and rights

  • You can edit your name and language in the app, turn live tracking on or off as a driver, and remove your trusted contact at any time.
  • To delete your account and personal data, email muhammadqasim0802@gmail.com from the address linked to your account. We delete the account within 30 days, except records we must keep for safety or legal reasons (for example reports of misconduct).
  • You can withdraw location or notification permissions in Android settings; some features will stop working.

6. Security

All traffic is encrypted in transit. Access to data in the database is restricted per user with row-level security; phone numbers and plate numbers are only readable through a function that checks for an accepted booking. Staff access is limited to what is needed to operate the service and handle reports.

7. Children

BlaCar is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date. Significant changes will also be announced in the app.
