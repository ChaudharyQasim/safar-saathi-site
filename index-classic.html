What you get

Built for long rides across Pakistan

Everything you need to share a 200 km ride with someone you have never met, without guesswork.

Verified drivers

Every driver scans their CNIC, licence and a live selfie with the camera, never a gallery upload, and is approved by our team before posting a ride.

Fair, transparent fares

Drivers pick a rate of Rs 8 to 14 per km. The price comes from the real road distance of the route, and what you see is what you pay.

Ladies-only rides

Women drivers can post rides reserved for women passengers. The rule is enforced by the server, not by request.

Family Circle

The moment a trip starts, your trusted contact gets a live link. They see when you are near and when you arrive, nothing more.

Private chat

One private thread per booking, open once the request is accepted. Phone numbers are shared only after the driver says yes.

Book part of a route

Driver going Lahore to Jhang via Faisalabad? Book just your leg and pay only for that distance.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is it free to use?

The app is free. A traveler pays a 10% service fee per seat, shown inside the price before booking. Drivers pay nothing.

How do I pay the fare?

For now the fare is settled on the trip. In-app payment is coming and will be announced in the app.

Can the driver see my number?

Only after accepting your request. Before that no number, no plate, nothing is shared either way.

Can I ask for a lower price?

Yes, down to half the listed price. The offer is for your seat only; the driver accepts or declines.

Which cities?

112 cities across Punjab, Sindh, KP, Balochistan, Gilgit-Baltistan and Azad Kashmir. Drivers add smaller towns on the way as stops.

Is it in Urdu?

The whole app is in Urdu and English, with a proper right-to-left layout and Nastaliq type.
